- **Value types covered:** Annex I §5.1 (Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons):
  - **5.1.6** Smoked meat and smoked meat products: benzo[a]pyrene ≤2.0 µg/kg, PAH4 sum ≤12.0 µg/kg.
  - **5.1.7** Smoked fishery products (except 5.1.8 list): same 2.0 / 12.0 µg/kg; scope clarified —
    applies to muscle meat of fish (whole fish if eaten whole); for crustaceans, muscle meat from
    appendages/abdomen only (cephalothorax excluded).
  - **5.1.8** Special-case general limit (5.0 / 30.0 µg/kg) for smoked sprats/canned smoked sprats,
    smoked Baltic herring ≤14cm, katsuobushi, bivalve molluscs, and heat-treated meat products.
  - **Article 7(3)** — named-country derogation (no time limit) for *traditionally* smoked meat/meat
    products up to 5.0 / 30.0 µg/kg: Ireland, Croatia, Cyprus, Spain, Poland, Portugal (general);
    Latvia (pork, hot-smoked chicken, hot-smoked sausages, hot-smoked game); Slovakia (salted
    traditionally smoked meat, bacon, klobása sausage — defined as smoke generated by burning wood logs/
    sawdust/chips in a smokehouse); Finland (traditionally hot-smoked meat); Sweden (meat smoked over
    glowing wood or other plant materials).
  - **Article 7(4)** — same derogation structure for traditionally smoked fish/fishery products:
    Latvia (traditionally hot-smoked fish), Finland (traditionally hot-smoked small fish), Sweden (fish
    smoked over glowing wood/plant materials).
  - All derogations require continued PAH monitoring and "good smoking practices... where possible."
- **What is missing / did not parse:** Nothing numeric missing for the PAH sections. The 55-page

## Disagreements this source touches

This is the source-map's anchor for **G-C** (wood/meat pairing has no authority) and the note in §3.6:
the EU regulates the *outcome* (PAH in the finished smoked product) and separately the *liquid smoke
flavoring* (Regulation (EC) 2065/2003 + 1321/2013, not in this project's minimal 19 — explicitly deleted
per source-map §10 "מה נמחק"), but **no EU or US regulation specifies which wood species are permitted
for direct pit-smoking, and none names conifers/treated wood by name.** This fetch does not settle G-C;
it confirms the source map's existing conclusion that G-C remains an open craft-only gap. Nothing in
this document allows sharpening or settling any of the D-1..D-8 disagreements — it is cited in the
source map for smoke/PAH content only, not for any of the temperature/time/degree-hours disputes.
