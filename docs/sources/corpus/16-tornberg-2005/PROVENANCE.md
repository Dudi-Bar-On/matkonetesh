## Relevance to the 94°C brisket disagreement (flagged, not settled — see main report)

This source supplies the **mechanism**, not a verdict: collagen denatures and begins shrinking at
53–63°C. It does not by itself explain why traditional practice targets 94°C (203°F) rather than stopping
at 63°C — that gap is bridged only by texture/palatability claims (gelatinization continuing well above
63°C, per Modernist Cuisine and craft-BBQ sources) that this peer-reviewed abstract does not confirm or
deny. See the dedicated 94°C section in the task report.
