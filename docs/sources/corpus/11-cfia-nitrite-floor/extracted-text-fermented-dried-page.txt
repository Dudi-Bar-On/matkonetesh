## Clostridium botulinum Control
To prevent Clostridium botulinum outgrowth, manufacturers must add "nitrite/nitrate are added at a
minimum level of 100 ppm along with a minimum of 2.5% of salt."

## Staphylococcus aureus Control (NOT extracted as structured data — see note above)
S. aureus cannot multiply above 15.6C or once pH reaches 5.3. "Degree-hours" = (temperature in excess
of 15.6C) x hours. Acceptable limits: <665 when highest fermentation temp <33C; <555 when 33-37C; <500
when >37C. [These numbers are the CFIA-side unit conversion of FSIS's 1200/1000/900 degree-F-hours,
per source-map §8.3 — same requirement (AMI Foundation 1997), different unit system. Not restated as
this source's own structured data per scope.]

## E. coli O157:H7 and Salmonella Control (out of scope for this source)
Five compliance options, Option 1 (validated heat process) through Option 5 (challenge study, 5-log
reduction). Beef products require one of these options or lot testing of 30 finished samples.

## Dried Products Requirements (out of scope for this source)
Dried beef products require heat treatment before drying: 71C internal temperature for 15 seconds, or
validated equivalent.

## Shelf-Stable Criteria (out of scope for this source)
Shelf-stable without refrigeration: pH <=4.6 OR water activity <=0.85, OR pH <=5.3 at end of
fermentation with final water activity <=0.90.
