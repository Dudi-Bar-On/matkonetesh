What is the safe temperature to cook organ meat?

Knowledge Article
Organs, such as kidney, liver, stomach, tongue, and tripe, from red meats (beef, veal, pork, or lamb) should be cooked to a minimum internal temperature of 160 °F as measured with a food thermometer. Poultry livers and other giblets should reach a safe minimum internal temperature of 165 °F as measured with a food thermometer.
